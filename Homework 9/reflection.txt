In this project, I essentially coded my program with a lot of trial and error and reading up on the p5play website, simply trying to piece together things that would work from both that and the help I received from Mr. Cassens throughout said coding process. My project did not use generative AI in any way - I could see it being used to help generate a list of sprite images or a sprite sheet, or being used to help iron out smaller issues such as the odd collision system p5play employs.
