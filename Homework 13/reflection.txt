In this project, my process was to build off of what I already had from Homework 12 - using translations, pushes and pops, and the like, and going down the list to make everything work. I found a teapot model online using Streamfab, imported it into my project, and changed two of the textures of my shapes so that all five differed from each other. Figuring out the arrays was difficult, but thanks to some help from both Michael and an online tutorial, I managed to import a form of class inheritance into my project, thus allowing for each shape to have its own class, be easily implanted into a spot in my shape array, and be likewise easily displayed thanks to that same array. Lastly, the motion/interaction was simple, as I just had to modify the x and y coordinates of two of the shapes within a mousePressed() class.

Aside from a question to Michael and a couple online tutorials, plus the notes provided to me in this class, no other tools were used to help augment my learning, including generative AI.
